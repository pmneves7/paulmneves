Alternative twin domains

Twins the lattice permits for this space group. Obverse/reverse twins fill in otherwise-absent reflections; merohedral twins keep the same reflection conditions but overlap reflections (biasing intensities). Pseudo-merohedral twins from accidental metric coincidences are not considered.

    Scattering angle definitions

    When a scattering plane is provided, ω is the smallest signed crystal rotation about the out-of-plane axis that satisfies cos(φ + ω) = −sin(θ)/cos(χ), including both symmetric detector branches (φ = in-plane angle, χ = out-of-plane angle, θ = Bragg angle).

    Intensity

    Count-rate estimates use ideal coherent scattering from the loaded unit cell and do not include absorption, sample shape, detector solid angle, efficiency, resolution, mosaicity, or extinction.

    Structure factors use room-temperature atomic scattering factors and neutron scattering lengths with no Debye–Waller (thermal) factor and no anomalous dispersion — so X-ray F_imag is always 0. The reported |F| and intensities are ideal-lattice estimates.

    Magnetic order estimator

    Simple collinear estimate for neutron satellites at τ ± k. Moments are taken equal and in phase on the selected site or element; the intensity includes a 2/3 orientation average.

    Scattering plane

    Enter two non-collinear HKLs in the scattering plane to calculate a signed out-of-plane angle. The in-plane angle is measured from the first HKL toward the second. HKL 1 defines the in-plane reference axis used for φ and ω (not the incident beam direction).
